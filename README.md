# Intervalify

Intervalify is a musical ear-training game that helps you recognize the distance between two notes. Listen, choose an interval, and build familiarity with each sound through short, repeatable levels.

[Play Intervalify](https://intervalify.app/)

## Listen at your own pace

Each level has ten questions. You'll hear two notes played upwards, downwards, or together, then choose the interval you heard. Replay them as often as you like: there is no timer, and you can answer whenever you're ready.

Feedback shows the correct interval after each answer. A row of green and red markers keeps track of your answers as you go.

## A journey through 100 levels

Five chapters take you from familiar intervals to closer distinctions, different instrument sounds, and intervals beyond an octave. Each level has its own combination of sounds and listening challenges.

- **5 correct answers** earn one star and unlock the next level.
- **8 correct answers** earn two stars.
- **10 correct answers** earn three stars.

Collect 20 stars in a chapter to open the next one. You can revisit earlier levels at any time; your best score and stars are kept.

## Made for everyday practice

- **English and German**, with musical interval names in both languages.
- **Varied instrument sounds** to help you recognize intervals across different tones and registers.
- **Automatic progress saving** on the device and browser you use.
- **Offline play** once the web app displays “Ready to play offline.”
- **No account, ads, or time pressure.**

Progress stays on your device rather than syncing between devices. Clearing the app or website's stored data removes it. Offline files may also be removed by your browser.

The shared codebase supports web, Android and iOS. You can play in your browser at [intervalify.app](https://intervalify.app/).

## About the project

Created by **Tim Fischer**, Intervalify is built around a simple practice loop: listen, make a choice, learn from the answer, and try again.

The application uses TypeScript, React Native and Expo. Setup, development commands and deployment instructions live in the [developer guide](DEVELOPMENT.md). More detailed notes cover [level design](LEVEL_DESIGN.md) and [language support](LOCALIZATION.md).
